1. **Supersede** (lykos/seal/create.ts). Change both: the previous seals are every active seal at the writer commit (no branch filter), skipping untrusted, invalid-signature and base-changed ones exactly as today. Union their `sealed_paths` into the new seal, minus any `.lykos/seals/*.json` path other than the new seal's own. Prune their seal files (add them to `prunedPaths`) so the new seal commit deletes them; `isPrunedBySealCommit` in active.ts then stops reporting them. 3. Update the seal reference doc (find it under docs/reference/) with one or two sentences: a seal made on a branch of its own supersedes the active seals it was made from, keeping every path they sealed.
